Version 1.2.0

Release notes

4 updates
01

Help improve ResistGate

New

A direct way to send product feedback from the extension.

Added a small feedback button in the popup and options page that opens a dedicated survey. The answers now go straight into the dashboard so the product can improve from real user input.

02

Pro settings page fixes

Fix

The Pro area now stays behind entitlement.

Fixed the bug that let free users wander into Pro-only settings. The options page now gates the Pro tab and keeps paid controls locked until the account is actually activated.

03

Welcome page redesign

Polish

A cleaner first-run experience for new users.

The welcome page has been refreshed so new installs feel less stale and more intentional. It now introduces ResistGate with clearer guidance and a better sense of what to do first.

04

Graduated challenges

Free

Choose the level of friction that fits the moment.

Free users can now start with an Easy short character sequence, step up to Moderate one or two sentence prompts, or use Hard mode with the original five-paragraph challenge drawn from a larger bank of unique paragraphs. The idea is simple: build discipline without burning out.
